When I started writing about cryptocurrency in 2020, 
I still carried a naive assumption about the size of this economy.
 I thought people were generally decent and the grifter class was a small pool of degenerates with rotten moral character, preying on those made vulnerable by the material conditions of our time.

I was very wrong. The grift economy is massive.
 More disturbing still, it is participatory. A large and growing share of the population now appears willing to devote every waking hour to fleecing their fellow man as a career choice. They stream, post, recruit, promote, refer, astroturf, and close. They turn every friendship into a lead and every conversation into a qualifying call. They do not clock out because the market follows them into bed.
 The smartphone is a shop counter that sleeps beside their head.
